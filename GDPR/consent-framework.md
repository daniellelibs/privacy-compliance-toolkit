# GDPR Consent Framework

**Explicit Consent Model per GDPR Art. 7**

---

## 1. GDPR Consent Principles

- ✅ **Granular:** Separate consent by purpose
- ✅ **Explicit:** Affirmative action required (opt-in)
- ✅ **Informed:** Clear information provided
- ✅ **Free:** No coercion, easily revocable
- ✅ **Specific:** Purpose clearly identified

---

## 4. Consent Revocation

**Simplified Process:**

1. Data subject requests revocation via email/form
2. Respond within 5 business days
3. Delete data within 30 days
4. Confirm deletion in writing
