# GDPR Compliance Checklist

**General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) - EU 2016/679**

Evaluate your organization for GDPR compliance. Mark as ✅ Compliant, ⚠️ Partially Compliant, or ❌ Non-Compliant.

---

## 📋 Governance & Structure

- [ ] **Data Protection Officer (DPO) Designated**
  - Formally appointed and contact accessible
  - Independence assured
  - Adequate resources provided

- [ ] **Data Mapping (Data Inventory)**
  - Complete inventory of processing activities
  - Documentation of data categories
  - Data lifecycle mapped

- [ ] **Records of Processing Activity**
  - Maintained for controller and processor
  - Accessible for supervision
  - Regularly updated

- [ ] **GDPR-Compliant Privacy Policy**
  - Articles 13/14 transparency implemented
  - Clear and concise language
  - Published and accessible

---

## 🔐 Security & Technical Measures

- [ ] **Encryption in Transit & at Rest**
  - TLS 1.2+ for transmissions
  - Data at rest encrypted
  - Keys managed per ISO 27001

- [ ] **Access Control (RBAC)**
  - Principle of least privilege
  - Function segregation
  - Quarterly access reviews

- [ ] **Strong Authentication**
  - MFA for administrative access
  - Passwords per NIST guidelines
  - Account lockout after failed attempts

- [ ] **Audit Logs**
  - All access recorded
  - Minimum 1 year retention
  - Anomaly analysis implemented

- [ ] **Backup & Disaster Recovery**
  - Daily/weekly backups
  - Quarterly restoration testing
  - RTO/RPO documented

---

## 📊 Consent & Transparency

- [ ] **Explicit Consent (When Necessary)**
  - Granular by purpose
  - Opt-in, not pre-selected
  - Easily revocable

- [ ] **Information (Art. 13/14)**
  - Controller identity
  - Processing purposes
  - Legal basis
  - Data subject rights
  - Retention period
  - Profiling information

- [ ] **Clear & Transparent Language**
  - No vague terms
  - Accessible to data subject
  - No unnecessary technical jargon

---

## 👤 Data Subject Rights

Mechanisms to exercise:

- [ ] **Right of Access (Art. 15)**
  - Response within 30 days
  - Common electronic format
  - Readable copy of data

- [ ] **Right of Rectification (Art. 16)**
  - Process to correct data
  - Notification to recipients if applicable

- [ ] **Right to Erasure (Art. 17)**
  - Deletion in specific contexts
  - Notification to data recipients

- [ ] **Right to Restrict (Art. 18)**
  - Data marked for restriction
  - Limited processing after restriction

- [ ] **Right to Portability (Art. 20)**
  - Data in structured/common format
  - Direct transmission if viable

- [ ] **Right to Object (Art. 21)**
  - Processing cessation
  - Clear process implemented

- [ ] **Rights Related to Automated Decisions (Art. 22)**
  - Logic explained
  - Right to human review
  - Right not to be subject solely to profiling

- [ ] **Breach Notification (Art. 33/34)**
  - Authority notified within 72h
  - Data subject notified without delay

---

## 🏢 Consent & Legal Bases

Clear documentation for each processing:

- [ ] **Consent** (Art. 6(a)) - when applicable
- [ ] **Contract** (Art. 6(b)) - when applicable
- [ ] **Legal Obligation** (Art. 6(c)) - when applicable
- [ ] **Vital Interest** (Art. 6(d)) - when applicable
- [ ] **Public Task** (Art. 6(e)) - when applicable
- [ ] **Legitimate Interest** (Art. 6(f)) - with Balancing Test

---

## 🔄 Processing & Retention

- [ ] **Data Minimization (Art. 5)**
  - Only necessary data
  - Relevance reviewed

- [ ] **Specific Purpose (Art. 5)**
  - New consent for new purpose
  - Compatibility documented

- [ ] **Retention Limitation (Art. 5)**
  - Written policy
  - Automatic deletion when possible
  - Legal exceptions considered

- [ ] **Integrity & Confidentiality (Art. 5)**
  - Protection against loss/damage
  - Restricted access

---

## 👥 Third Parties & Transfers

- [ ] **Processing Agreement (Art. 28)**
  - Contract with all processors
  - Security/confidentiality clauses
  - Sub-processors authorized

- [ ] **Third-Party Sharing**
  - Documented by purpose
  - Consent/legal basis obtained
  - Data subject transparency

- [ ] **International Transfers (Cap. V)**
  - Only to adequate countries OR
  - With appropriate guarantees (SCCs, BCRs)
  - Documentation assessment

- [ ] **Processor Agreement**
  - Rights/obligations clear
  - Termination and data deletion

---

## 📱 Advanced Technical Compliance

- [ ] **Privacy by Design & Default (Art. 25)**
  - Implemented from conception
  - Security standards incorporated

- [ ] **Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)**
  - Conducted for high-risk processing
  - Documented and maintained
  - Supervisor consulted if necessary

- [ ] **Impact Related to Profiling**
  - Assessed in DPIA
  - Measures to minimize risk

- [ ] **Certifications & Seals**
  - Considered to demonstrate compliance
  - Applicable Code of Conduct

---

## 📢 Incidents & Notification

- [ ] **Incident Response**
  - Documented procedure
  - Responsible team
  - Regular testing

- [ ] **Incident Registry**
  - Date/time
  - Facts
  - Effects
  - Actions taken

- [ ] **Authority Notification (Art. 33)**
  - Without delay, maximum 72h
  - Single authority contact

- [ ] **Data Subject Notification (Art. 34)**
  - When high risk to rights
  - Incident description
  - Mitigating measures
  - DPO contact

---

## 🎓 Administrative Compliance

- [ ] **Staff Training (Art. 32)**
  - Annual for all with access
  - Documented
  - Security awareness

- [ ] **Confidentiality Agreement**
  - Signed by employees
  - Clear obligations

- [ ] **Compliance Documentation**
  - Records for 3+ years
  - Supervisor-ready

- [ ] **Annual Report**
  - Compliance reviewed
  - Gaps identified
  - Remediation plan

---

## 🔍 Monitoring

- [ ] **Internal Audits**
  - Annually or per risk
  - Documented

- [ ] **External Review**
  - Considered for large volumes
  - Recommended every 2 years

- [ ] **Compliance Metrics**
  - Tracked
  - Reported to leadership
