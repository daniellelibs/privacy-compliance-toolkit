# LGPD Compliance Checklist (English Version)

**Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD) - Law nº 13.709/2018**

Evaluate your organization for LGPD compliance. Mark each item as ✅ Compliant, ⚠️ Partially Compliant, or ❌ Non-Compliant.

---

## 📋 Governance & Structure

- [ ] **Appointment of Data Protection Officer (DPO)**
  - Formally designated responsible party
  - Accessible contact for authorities and data subjects
  
- [ ] **Personal Data Mapping**
  - Complete inventory of collected data
  - Documentation of data sources
  
- [ ] **Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)**
  - Conducted for high-risk processing
  - Documented and reviewed periodically
  
- [ ] **Formal Privacy Policy**
  - Published and accessible
  - Updated as operational changes occur
  
- [ ] **Record of Processing Activities**
  - Kept current
  - Available for audits

---

## 🔐 Data Security

- [ ] **Data Encryption in Transit**
  - HTTPS/TLS implemented for all transmissions
  - Secure channels for data access
  
- [ ] **Data Encryption at Rest**
  - Sensitive data encrypted in storage
  - Keys managed appropriately
  
- [ ] **Access Control (RBAC)**
  - Role-based access
  - Principle of least privilege implemented
  
- [ ] **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**
  - Implemented for personal data access
  - Required for employees
  
- [ ] **Audit Logs**
  - Recording all personal data access
  - Retention of at least 6 months
  
- [ ] **Network Segmentation**
  - Personal data isolated from non-essential systems
  - Firewalls properly configured
  
- [ ] **Incident Response Plan**
  - Documented procedures
  - Responsible team identified
  - Regular testing conducted

---

## 📊 Collection & Consent

- [ ] **Documented Explicit Consent**
  - Obtained BEFORE processing (when required)
  - Proof of consent stored
  - Easy withdrawal of consent
  
- [ ] **Clear & Transparent Language**
  - Information in accessible language
  - No vague or generic terms
  
- [ ] **Notice at Collection**
  - Information provided at collection time
  - Controller identification
  - Processing purpose
  
- [ ] **Opt-in vs Opt-out**
  - Consent is opt-in (explicit)
  - Not pre-selected/automatic

---

## 👤 Data Subject Rights

Processes implemented for:

- [ ] **Right of Access**
  - Requests answered within 15 days
  - Data in accessible format
  
- [ ] **Right of Rectification**
  - Incorrect data can be corrected
  - Process documented
  
- [ ] **Right of Erasure ("Right to Forgotten")**
  - Data can be deleted under certain conditions
  - Formal procedure established
  
- [ ] **Right of Portability**
  - Data in structured and readable format
  - Transfer to another provider possible
  
- [ ] **Right of Opposition**
  - Data subject can oppose processing
  - Process defined
  
- [ ] **Right to Non-Discrimination**
  - Data use does not result in discrimination
  - Automated processing does not harm rights
  
- [ ] **Right to Explanation (Automated Decisions)**
  - Automated decisions explained to subject
  - Right to human review
  
- [ ] **Right to Information**
  - Data subject informed about processing
  - Clear communications maintained

---

## 🤝 Legal Bases

Clear documentation of:

- [ ] **Consent** (when applicable)
- [ ] **Contract Execution** (when applicable)
- [ ] **Legal Obligation** (when applicable)
- [ ] **Life Protection** (when applicable)
- [ ] **Legitimate Interest** (when applicable and justified)
- [ ] **Public Interest** (when applicable)

Each processing has documented and justified legal basis.

---

## 🏢 International Transfers

- [ ] **Transfers Outside Brazil**
  - Only to countries with adequate protection OR
  - With appropriate contractual guarantees
  
- [ ] **Standard Contractual Clauses**
  - Implemented when necessary
  - Documented and kept current
  
- [ ] **Transfer Approval**
  - Risk evaluated beforehand
  - Compliance assured

---

## 🔄 Processing & Retention

- [ ] **Data Minimization**
  - Only necessary data collected
  - Periodic relevance review
  
- [ ] **Specific Purpose**
  - Data used only for declared purposes
  - Purpose change requires new consent
  
- [ ] **Justified Data Retention**
  - Retention policy documented
  - Data deleted after necessity
  - Legal exceptions considered
  
- [ ] **Data Quality**
  - Data accurate and up-to-date
  - Verification process implemented

---

## 👥 Third Parties & Sharing

- [ ] **Processor Engagement**
  - Data Processing Agreement in place
  - Security and confidentiality clauses
  
- [ ] **Third-Party Sharing**
  - Documented and justified
  - Consent obtained when necessary
  
- [ ] **Sub-processors**
  - List maintained current
  - Consent for changes (if necessary)
  
- [ ] **Confidentiality Clause**
  - All suppliers have signed
  - Clear obligations defined

---

## 📱 Technical Compliance

- [ ] **Privacy by Design**
  - Data protection considered from design
  - Implemented in new systems
  
- [ ] **Security Updates**
  - Patches applied regularly
  - Vulnerabilities monitored
  
- [ ] **Backup & Recovery**
  - Regular backups performed
  - Business continuity plan in place
  
- [ ] **Security Testing**
  - Penetration tests conducted annually
  - Vulnerabilities identified and remediated

---

## 📢 Incident Notification

- [ ] **Notification Procedure**
  - Documented and tested
  - Response time defined (up to 2 business days)
  
- [ ] **Incident Registry**
  - All incidents documented
  - Root cause analysis performed
  
- [ ] **Data Subject Communication**
  - Notification when rights affected
  - Clear and actionable information

---

## 📋 Administrative Compliance

- [ ] **Term of Responsibility**
  - Signed by employees with data access
  - Updated annually
  
- [ ] **LGPD Training**
  - Conducted for entire team
  - Documented with dates and participants
  
- [ ] **Compliance Documentation**
  - Records maintained for 3+ years
  - Ready for authorities
  
- [ ] **Compliance Report**
  - Annual report prepared
  - Approved by leadership

---

## 🔍 Monitoring & Audit

- [ ] **Internal Audits**
  - Conducted annually
  - Documented with remediation plan
  
- [ ] **Third-Party Review**
  - Considered for enhanced credibility
  - Recommended for large data volumes
  
- [ ] **Compliance Metrics**
  - Tracked regularly
  - Reported to leadership

---

## 📌 Notes

- **Scoring:** Use this checklist as baseline
- **Frequency:** Review quarterly
- **Responsible:** DPO or Compliance Officer
- **Evidence:** Maintain documentation for each ✅ item
