# Privacy & Compliance Toolkit

A comprehensive toolkit for data privacy audits and compliance implementation across **LGPD (Brazil)**, **GDPR (Europe)**, and **CCPA (California, USA)**.

## 🎯 Purpose

This toolkit provides:
- ✅ Compliance checklists by regulation
- ✅ Ready-to-use privacy policy templates
- ✅ Consent frameworks and impact assessment tools
- ✅ Comparative matrix across regulations

Ideal for **Compliance Officers**, **DPOs**, **GRC Specialists**, and **Security Teams** implementing data privacy compliance.

---

## 📋 Project Structure

### LGPD - Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (Brazil)
- **checklist-pt.md / checklist-en.md** - 50+ LGPD compliance items
- **privacy-policy-template-pt.md / privacy-policy-template-en.md** - LGPD-compliant Privacy Policy Template
- **assessment-pt.md / assessment-en.md** - LGPD Compliance Assessment Framework

- **checklist.md** - 60+ GDPR compliance items

- **assessment.md** - CCPA Compliance Assessment

### Tools
- **comparison-matrix.md** - LGPD vs GDPR vs CCPA Comparative Analysis

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Select your regulation** (LGPD, GDPR, or CCPA)
2. **Run the checklist** to identify compliance gaps
3. **Apply templates** in your organization
4. **Document with assessment** or DPIA tools
5. **Compare regulations** using the matrix for multi-jurisdictional requirements

---

## 📌 Key Differences At a Glance

| Aspect | LGPD | GDPR | CCPA |
|--------|------|------|------|
| **Jurisdiction** | Brazil | European Union | California, USA |
| **Scope** | Any data processing for BR residents | EU resident data | California resident data |
| **Max Fine** | Up to 2% of annual revenue | Up to €20M or 4% of revenue | Up to $7,500 per violation |
| **DPO Required** | No | Yes (certain cases) | No |
| **Data Subject Rights** | 9 main rights | 9+ main rights | 4 main rights |
