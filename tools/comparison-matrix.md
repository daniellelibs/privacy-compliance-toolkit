# Comparative Matrix: LGPD vs GDPR vs CCPA

---

## Overview

| Aspect | LGPD | GDPR | CCPA |
|--------|------|------|------|
| **Jurisdiction** | Brazil | European Union | California |
| **Effective Date** | 2020 | 2018 | 2020 |
| **Extraterritorial Application** | Yes, BR residents | Yes, EU residents | Yes, CA residents |

---

## Consent

| Aspect | LGPD | GDPR | CCPA |
|--------|------|------|------|
| **Consent Required?** | No (multiple bases) | No (multiple bases) | No (multiple bases) |
| **Opt-In or Opt-Out?** | Opt-in when required | Opt-in (required for marketing) | Opt-out (original) / Opt-in (CPRA) |
| **Granularity** | By purpose | Very granular | General |
| **Easy Revocation?** | Yes | Yes | Yes |

---

## Data Subject/Consumer Rights

| Right | LGPD | GDPR | CCPA |
|------|------|------|------|
| **Access** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| **Rectification** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| **Deletion** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| **Portability** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| **Opposition** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes (Opt-out) |
| **Non-Discrimination** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes (CPRA) |
| **Explanation of Automated Decisions** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |

---

## Security & Protection

| Aspect | LGPD | GDPR | CCPA |
|--------|------|------|------|
| **Encryption Required?** | Recommended | Recommended | Recommended |
| **DPIA/Assessment Required?** | AIPD for high risk | DPIA mandatory | Not required |
| **DPO Required?** | No | Yes (public/large scale) | No |
| **Breach Notification** | Yes, 2 business days | Yes, 72h | Yes, immediate |

---

## Fines & Penalties

| Level | LGPD | GDPR | CCPA |
|-------|------|------|------|
| **Max Fine** | 2% of annual revenue | €20M or 4% of revenue | $7,500/violation |
| **Min Fine** | Not specified | Structured | Not specified |
| **Criminal?** | No | No | Yes (CPRA) |

---

## Sensitive Data

| Category | LGPD | GDPR | CCPA |
|----------|------|------|------|
| **Biometric** | Special protection | Art. 9 (prohibited) | Protection (CPRA) |
| **Genetic** | Special protection | Art. 9 (prohibited) | Protection (CPRA) |
| **Health** | Special protection | Art. 9 (prohibited) | Protection (CPRA) |
| **Ethnic/Race** | Special protection | Art. 9 (prohibited) | Protection (CPRA) |

---

## International Transfers

| Method | LGPD | GDPR | CCPA |
|--------|------|------|------|
| **Adequate Country** | Yes | Yes | N/A |
| **Standard Contractual Clauses** | Yes | Yes (SCCs) | N/A |
| **Binding BCR** | Possible | Yes | N/A |

---

## Compliance Reporting

| Item | LGPD | GDPR | CCPA |
|------|------|------|------|
| **Maintain Records** | Yes, 3+ years | Yes, 3+ years | Yes, as needed |
| **Mandatory Audit** | No | No | No (internal recommended) |
| **Transparency** | Required | Required | Required |

---

## Practical Application

### Example 1: International E-commerce

**Collecting data from:**
- Brazilian → Apply LGPD
- European → Apply GDPR
- Californian → Apply CCPA

**Action:** Implement all three policies

---

### Example 2: Email Marketing

**LGPD:** Explicit consent OR justified legitimate interest  
**GDPR:** Explicit consent required  
**CCPA:** "Do Not Sell" opt-out available

---

## Recommendations

### For Global Companies

1. **Implement GDPR** → Most rigorous standard
2. **Add LGPD** → Brazil market
3. **Add CCPA** → USA/California market

### For Startups

1. **Start with GDPR** → Best practice
2. **Adapt as you grow** → LGPD, CCPA

### Effort Level

- LGPD: Medium
- GDPR: High
- CCPA: Medium-Low
