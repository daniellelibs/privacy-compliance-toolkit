## Your CCPA Rights

### Right to Know
You can request:
- Categories of data collected
- Purposes of use
- Categories of third parties receiving data

### Right to Delete
You can request deletion of data, with exceptions (e.g., mandatory legal records)

### Right to Opt-Out
You can opt out of data "sales" or "sharing" (per CCPA/CPRA)

### Right to Non-Discrimination
We do not discriminate against consumers exercising CCPA rights
