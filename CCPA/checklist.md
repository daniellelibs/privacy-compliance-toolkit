# CCPA Compliance Checklist

**California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) - CA Civil Code § 1798.100 et seq.**

---

## 📋 Governance & Structure

- [ ] **Published Privacy Policy**
  - Per CCPA Section 1798.100
  - Updated annually
  - Data categories described

- [ ] **Privacy Contact Designated**
  - Public email/phone
  - Responses within 45 days max

- [ ] **Data Sale Registry**
  - Maintained if data is sold
  - Documented and reviewable

- [ ] **Data Category Mapped**
  - Personal
  - Commercial
  - Biometric
  - Professional
  - Educational
  - Inferences

---

## 🔐 Security

- [ ] **Data Security Implemented**
  - Encryption
  - Access control
  - Monitoring
  - Incident response

---

## 📊 Collection & Disclosure

- [ ] **Notice at Collection Point**
  - Privacy policy link
  - Clear data collection statement

- [ ] **"Do Not Sell My Personal Information" Link**
  - Visible on homepage
  - Easy to find and use

- [ ] **Rights Described in Policy**
  - Access
  - Deletion
  - Opt-out
  - Non-discrimination

---

## 👤 Consumer Rights

- [ ] **Access Process (Section 1798.100)**
  - Response within 45 days
  - Readable format
  - May deny if frivolous

- [ ] **Deletion Process (Section 1798.105)**
  - Response within 45 days
  - Notify processors
  - Exceptions documented

- [ ] **Opt-Out Right (Section 1798.120)**
  - "Do Not Sell" link active
  - Change effective within 15 days
  - Applies to future sales

- [ ] **Non-Discrimination Right (Section 1798.125)**
  - Equal price/quality
  - No punishment for exercising rights

---

## 🤝 Third Parties

- [ ] **Processor Contracts**
  - CCPA Data Processing Addendum
  - Use restrictions documented

- [ ] **Data Transfer on Business Sale**
  - Consent documented OR
  - Transfer permitted under CCPA

---

## 📢 Breach Notification

- [ ] **Security Breaches**
  - Immediate notification
  - Specific information provided
  - Consumer rights explained

---

## ⚖️ Compliance

- [ ] **Internal Audits**
  - Annually
  - Gaps identified

- [ ] **Documentation**
  - Privacy policy updated
  - Process records maintained
