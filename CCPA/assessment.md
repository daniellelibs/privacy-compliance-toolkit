# CCPA Assessment

Quickly evaluate CCPA compliance.

## Quick Checklist

- [ ] Privacy Notice published and updated?
- [ ] "Do Not Sell My Information" link visible?
- [ ] Access/deletion processes implemented?
- [ ] Processor contracts per CCPA?
- [ ] Breaches notifiable within 30 days?
- [ ] Annual internal audit conducted?

**Score:** _____ / 6

**Status:** 
- 6/6 = ✅ Compliant
- 4-5/6 = ⚠️ Partially
- 4/6 = ❌ Non-compliant
